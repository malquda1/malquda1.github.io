AI Research & Applied Machine Learning

Building intelligent systems that integrate AI into everyday collaboration.

I am a Principal Applied Scientist / Manager at Microsoft within the Collab AI Research group (AI for Meetings & Collaborative Apps). My work focuses on bridging foundational AI research and human-computer interaction to create intelligent, user-centric experiences that elevate productivity and decision-making at scale.

With over a decade of experience across industry and academia—including leading Generative AI initiatives at Best Buy, data science at IKEA Digital, and earning my Ph.D. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) from Temple University—I specialize in translating high-level ambiguous challenges into production-grade LLM, RAG, and multimodal machine learning systems.

Featured Research

arXiv 2026 · Enterprise AI Agents June 2026

A Single Rewrite Suffices: Empirical Lessons from Production Skill Description Optimization

Yangqiaoyu Zhou, Mohammad Alqudah, Kwei-Herng Lai, Aaron Halfaker, Yingqi Xiong, Yaar Harari

Investigates automated skill description optimization pipelines for enterprise AI agents, addressing skill collision in LLM query routing and demonstrating through systematic ablation that a single targeted LLM rewrite using false-positive and false-negative cases captures the bulk of production routing improvements.

RevieWeaver: Weaving Together Review Insights by Leveraging LLMs and Semantic Similarity

Jiban Adhikary, Mohammad Alqudah, Arun Palghat Udayashankar

Introduced a unified, scalable framework capable of distilling key product insights from up to 30 million customer reviews using semantic similarity, union-find clustering, and curated context grounding for cost-efficient, unbiased LLM summarization.
